<h1 class="page-title">Teaching</h1>

<div class="course-entry">
  <strong>HIDS 431: Introduction to Natural Language Processing in Health</strong><br>
  Years Taught: Fall 2020, Fall 2021<br><br>
  Presents a broad overview of methodologies to analyze and mine biomedical text automatically, exposing students to common and state-of-the-art tools, algorithms, and techniques for extracting content and knowledge from biomedical texts, with emphasis on healthcare and clinical narratives.
</div>

<div class="course-entry">
  <strong>HIDS 422: Ontologies in Health</strong><br>
  Years Taught: Spring 2020, Spring 2021, Spring 2022<br><br>
  Covers essential concepts of ontologies, building ontologies, and knowledge representation in healthcare and biomedical research &mdash; multidisciplinary at the intersection of philosophy, mathematical logic, AI, and informatics, including coding systems used in biomedical research.
</div>
