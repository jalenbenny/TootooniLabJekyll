  Vancomycin dosing errors can harm kidneys or drive antimicrobial resistance. Developed a Bayesian algorithm to estimate vancomycin AUC/MIC from patient-specific concentration-time data and dosing history, optimizing ICU dosing regimens to balance efficacy against nephrotoxicity risk. Validated against commercial pharmacokinetic tools (DoseMe, PrecisePK).
